# CodexMonitor 0.7.17

Restore the compact menu-bar layout from 0.7.15 and add ScapeTZ-inspired glass surfaces.

- Restore the 300-point panel, compact quota cards, smaller typography, and original account headers and footer.
- Use the native popover background and Liquid Glass surfaces for accounts, reset forecasts, reset details, and quota-limit notices on macOS 26 and later, with a material fallback on earlier systems and an opaque surface for Reduce Transparency.
- Keep reset-credit expiration dates tied to the relative or absolute time preference. Absolute mode shows the local calendar date, and relative mode shows the time remaining until expiration.
